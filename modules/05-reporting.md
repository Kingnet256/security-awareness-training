# Module 5 — Reporting an Incident

## The one idea
**Fast reporting beats perfect reporting.** You will never be in trouble
for reporting something that turned out to be harmless.

## Report immediately if you
- Clicked a suspicious link or entered a password on an odd page.
- Approved an MFA prompt you didn't start.
- Sent money/data because of an urgent request that now feels off.
- Lost a device with work access, or spotted anything unusual.

## How to report (fill in for your org)
- **Primary:** email `security@yourorg.example` / IT helpdesk.
- **Urgent / out of hours:** phone `+__ ___ ____`.
- **Include:** what happened, when, and any screenshots (don't forward the
  live malicious attachment).

## Why speed matters
Most breaches get worse over hours, not seconds. A password changed and an
account locked in the first ten minutes usually stops the whole chain.

> Reporting is a **contribution to team safety**, not an admission of fault.
